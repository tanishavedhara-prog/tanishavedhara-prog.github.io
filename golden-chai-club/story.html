the story

New city, new chapter

After pivoting my career and moving to Dubai, I went looking for a community where I could keep learning, meet like-minded women and grow my mindset — women who uplift each other because they're doing something, and who love to collaborate.

I couldn't find it. So I sent out a few requests for a coffee meetup.

More and more women with the same energy kept showing up. And here we are — making new connections over activities and fun, while everyone leaves her chai having learned something about her own wellness. That's the core.
