Our story · Meet the founder

It all started with a coffee.

“I was looking for a community that didn't exist yet — so, one coffee invitation at a time, we built it together.”

I'm Tanisha — a research scientist and health coach, and the founder of the Golden Chai Club.

01

A new chapter, a new city

My career began in research science, and over time I found myself drawn to the human side of health — how we eat, move, rest and think. So I did something both terrifying and exciting: I pivoted. I became a health coach, and around the same time I packed up my life and moved to Dubai.

New city. New career. New me. Anyone who has reinvented themselves knows how thrilling — and how lonely — that can feel.

02

Looking for my people

I went searching for a community where I could keep learning, meet like-minded women and keep growing my mindset. I wanted to be around women who uplift each other because they're doing something — building, creating, healing, starting over — and who love to collaborate rather than compete.

I found networking events, and I found social circles. But a space that brought it all together — knowledge, warmth, wellness and real connection? That, I couldn't find.

03

A few coffee invitations

So I started small. I sent out a few requests for a coffee meetup. Just a table, a few cups and some honest conversation.

Then something wonderful happened. More women came — women with the same energy, the same curiosity, the same quiet longing for something deeper. One coffee became another, and another, until the table simply wasn't big enough anymore.

04

And here we are

Today, the Golden Chai Club is a growing circle of women making new connections over activities, workshops, beach walks, breathwork, bingo cards and plenty of laughter.

But at its heart it's simple: we have fun, and every woman leaves her cup of chai having learned something new about her own wellness. That's the core of everything we do.

“Come for the chai and the conversation. Leave a little more educated, connected and inspired.”
Tanisha Vedhara · Founder
What the club stands for

Connection, with a purpose.

Everything we create is built around three simple promises — the same things I was searching for when I first arrived.

Learn

Every gathering leaves you with something real — on wellness, health, mindset and growth — from women who know their stuff.

Connect

Uplifting, like-minded women who are building something of their own, and who show up for each other.

Collaborate

Friendships that turn into ideas, partnerships and projects. When women pool their energy, everyone rises.

An invitation

If you feel aligned…

If you've read this far and found yourself nodding, you're probably one of us. Whether you've just landed in Dubai, are in the middle of your own reinvention, or simply want to be around women who lift you higher — there's a seat at the table for you.

I would genuinely love to meet you.

— Tanisha
