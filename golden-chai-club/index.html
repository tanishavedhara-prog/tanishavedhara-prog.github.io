What we're about

Real women. Real tables. Real conversations.

Somewhere between the school run, the boardroom and everything we carry, we stopped making time for each other. The Golden Chai Club is where we make it again.

We are a curated community of women at every stage of life — founders, mothers, creatives, professionals and women in the middle of reinventing who they are. We meet over chai in beautiful spaces for intimate tables, expert-led sessions, breathwork, creative workshops and sunrise walks by the sea.

No networking pressure, no performance — just warmth, curiosity and women who genuinely show up for one another.

How we gather

Every gathering feels different.

From candid panel conversations to silent breathwork, playful icebreakers to beach walks — every experience is designed to leave you a little lighter and a lot more connected.

THE TABLE

Honest conversations

Intimate talks and panels with inspiring women on reinvention, resilience, visibility and the stories we rarely say out loud.

THE PAUSE

Breath & wellbeing

Guided breathwork, nervous-system resets and soul-and-body sessions — a rare hour that is entirely yours.

THE SPARK

Play & connection

Reinvention Bingo and thoughtful icebreakers that turn a room of strangers into friends before the chai goes cold.

THE MOVE

Walks & workshops

Beach walks, metabolic socials and hands-on creative workshops — because growth happens in the body, too.
